The workflow

From motor list to finished panel

Each tool feeds the next. Start by listing your motors, let the cable sizer check your runs, then generate the operator layout — ready to cut, print or drop into your schematic.

1

Size your motors

Add up to 10 motors. Pick DOL, star-delta, soft start or VSD and get the contactor, overload and short-circuit protection to your standard — plus the full aux kit and project BOM.

2

Size your cables

Check each run against BS 7671 — ampacity, volt drop, derating and starting factors across SWA, Y-CY and LAPP. Pass / marginal / fail on every conductor size, no guesswork.

3

Build the mimic

Tick the devices each motor needs and the panel mimic builds itself — start/stop, lamps, selectors, E-stop. Lay it out, then export a print-ready PDF or a DXF with a true CutContour layer.

Who it's for

Built by an electrician,
for the people on the tools

Sizing a control panel usually means jumping between a spreadsheet, a manufacturer catalogue and a cable table — then redrawing the operator layout by hand. PanelXY pulls it into one place.

Whether you're a panel builder quoting a job, a maintenance sparky swapping a starter, or a design engineer checking a run, the tools give you defensible numbers to a recognised standard and artwork you can actually use.

It's free, it runs in any browser, and nothing you enter ever leaves your machine — so it's safe to use on client work.
